Build a dossier

Show a reviewer (an exchange, an OTC desk, a lender) where your shielded ZEC came from and that you can spend it, without handing over your viewing key. You list the transactions of the funds; this page builds a dossier of claims about them, which the reviewer checks against the chain.

Loading the builder…

Your viewing key stays in this page

  • The key is used here, by the builder running in your browser (WebAssembly). It is never sent: not to this site, not to the node.
  • Nothing is stored. The key field is cleared once the dossier is built, and closing the page forgets everything.
  • The page asks a public node for each transaction you list, by its id. The node learns those ids, and so could link them to you.
  • The dossier discloses only what its claims need. Read the summary before you share it.

The funds to explain

Or find them for me

This page reads the chain from a height you choose and tries your viewing key on every shielded output, here, to list the transactions that paid you or spent your notes. The node learns which heights you read, not your key or your transactions.

Each 1,000 blocks take about two seconds. The found ids replace the list above; you can remove any you do not want to explain.

How to find your transaction ids

Open your wallet's transaction history, open each transaction and copy its id (64 characters, also called the txid or transaction hash). Most wallets can also export the history as a CSV file, which lists the ids. With zcash-devtool, wallet list-tx prints them. Any order will do.

Control (optional): answer a reviewer's challenge

If the reviewer sent you a nonce: from this wallet, send any small amount to your own address with the nonce as the memo, wait for it to be mined, then enter both here. It proves you can spend these funds now.

What it discloses

    After the case: the nk in this dossier lets the reviewer see when any of its notes is spent, for as long as they keep it. Once the case is closed you may move the remaining funds to a fresh account in your wallet (with its own keys), whose notes this nk does not cover.

    What a dossier shows the reviewer

    • Where the funds entered your wallet, and what funded that transaction (its transparent inputs are public).
    • Each move of the funds within your wallet, and each payment you made from them (with a sender receipt).
    • With a control answer: that you could spend the funds after the reviewer's nonce was issued.
    • When any disclosed note is spent, now and later: the dossier's nk lets the reviewer compute these notes' nullifiers and watch for them on chain.

    What it does not hand over

    • Your viewing key: the reviewer cannot see your other payments, balance or history.
    • Any note or payment you do not list.
    • The ability to move funds: nothing here can spend.

    Privacy: the only requests this page makes outside this site are the transaction lookups, one per id you list, when you build. They carry the id and nothing else. The page stores nothing.
