Offline (optional): load the transactions from files

One file per transaction, named <txid>.hex, with the raw transaction in hex (from your own node, say). With files loaded, no node is asked: the claims are checked against the files, and whether those transactions are in the chain is not.

To check a claim, this page asks a public node for each transaction the dossier names, by its id: the node learns those ids, nothing else. The dossier itself is not sent anywhere.

      What this does not prove

        Terms used here
        Note
        A shielded output: an amount paid to an address, with a memo. A dossier discloses a note by its opening, which shows its transaction, amount, address and memo.
        Nullifier
        The value a transaction publishes when it spends a note. Only the key of the note's owner gives it, so a note's nullifier in a transaction shows that its owner spent it there.
        nk (nullifier key)
        Part of the holder's viewing key, in the dossier. With it this page computes the disclosed notes' nullifiers, and so finds where each was spent; it cannot spend, and it shows no other payment. It also lets whoever holds the dossier see when these notes are spent later, and anyone who ever paid the holder see when the notes they paid are spent.
        Explained (untraced)
        The claims add up when every payment's and the control's funds trace back, by path claims, to an origin claim, and no transaction paid from notes the dossier does not disclose. A note that no chain of claims leads back to an origin is untraced; the case is then amber, “funds not fully explained”.
        Consistent offline
        With transactions loaded from files, the claims are checked against those files only: whether the transactions are in the chain, and at which heights, is not. A holder can send fabricated files, so load only files you fetched from a node yourself.
        Origin
        Where the funds entered the holder's wallet, and what funded that transaction (its transparent inputs, or an undisclosed shielded sender).
        Path
        A disclosed note was spent in the transaction that created the next one: the funds moved within the wallet.
        Deposit
        A shielded payment the holder made, opened by a sender receipt, paid from disclosed notes.
        Transparent payment
        A payment the holder made to a transparent address (an exchange deposit, say), from disclosed notes. Funds that come back from that address show as “returned from claim #k”.
        Control
        A challenge transaction spent disclosed notes and paid the holder a note whose memo carries your nonce: the holder could spend those funds after you issued it.
        H₀ (issued at height)
        The chain height when you issued the nonce. A control transaction mined before it was not made in answer to your challenge, and fails.
        Verified
        The chain supports the claim.
        Not proven (unproven)
        Nothing in this dossier can show the claim, and waiting will not change that; its row says what would (an origin note is the holder's only once a later claim spends it).
        Not checked yet (not_checked)
        A transaction the claim rests on was not found, or is not mined yet: check again later.
        Failed
        The chain does not support the claim, or its data does not check: its row says why.
        TAZ
        Testnet coins, which have no value; ZEC on mainnet.

        After the case

        • Holder: the dossier's nk stays with the reviewer, and with anyone the dossier reaches. With it they see when each disclosed note is spent, past and future. More: anyone who ever paid you (an exchange that sent withdrawals to you, for example) and obtains the nk can see when every note they paid you is spent, past and future, not only the disclosed ones. Once the case is closed, move the remaining funds to a fresh account (a new account in your wallet, with its own keys): its notes are not covered by this nk.
        • Reviewer: keep the dossier file and the report (Download report, JSON) together in the case file. The report carries the dossier's sha256, so anyone can later confirm which dossier you reviewed and check it again with the same verifier (its sha256 is at the foot of this page).

        Challenge the holder

        A dossier's origin, path and deposit claims show what the holder's funds did. To see that they can spend them now, send them a fresh nonce and ask for a control claim that answers it. Generating a nonce also asks a public node for the chain's height (H₀), and nothing else.

        1. Send the holder this nonce.
        2. From the wallet that holds the funds, they send any amount (the smallest will do) to their own address, with the nonce as the memo. The transaction must spend notes the dossier discloses.
        3. They add that transaction to their dossier as the control claim (on the Build page: the nonce and the challenge transaction's id), and send you the new dossier.
        4. Open it here: the control claim must verify and answer this nonce.

        A case on screen is checked again when you change the nonce, the height or the deposit address. They are kept in this page's fields only, not stored: reloading or closing the page forgets them, so copy them into your case file.

        Privacy: this page asks a public node for each transaction the dossier names, only when you check one (none when you load the transactions from files), and for the chain's height when you generate a nonce; it sends nothing else. The node learns which transactions you looked up. The page stores nothing.
