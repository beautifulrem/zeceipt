    After the case
    
            
      
            Holder
            The dossier's nk stays with the reviewer and anyone the dossier reaches: with it they see when each disclosed note is spent, past and future, and anyone who ever paid the holder can see when the notes they paid are spent. Once the case is closed, move the remaining funds to a fresh account.
            
      
            Reviewer
            Keep the dossier file and the report (Download report) together in the case file. The report carries the dossier's sha256, so anyone can later confirm which dossier you reviewed and check it again with the same verifier (its sha256 is at the foot of this page).
            
    
            
  
            
  
            
  
            
  Terms used here
  
            
    
            Note
            A shielded output: an amount paid to an address, with a memo. A dossier discloses a note by its opening: its transaction, amount, address and memo.
            
    
            Nullifier
            The value a transaction publishes when it spends a note. Only the note owner's key gives it, so a note's nullifier in a transaction shows its owner spent it there.
            
    
            nk (nullifier key)
            Part of the holder's viewing key, in the dossier. With it this page finds where each disclosed note was spent; it cannot spend, and shows no other payment.
            
    
            Explained
            Every payment's and the control's funds trace back, by path claims, to an origin that names its source, and no transaction paid from notes the dossier does not disclose. Otherwise the case is “funds not fully explained”.
            
    
            Untraced
            A note no chain of path claims leads back to an origin claim.
            
    
            Origin
            Where the funds entered the holder's wallet, and what funded that transaction.
            
    
            Path
            A disclosed note was spent in the transaction that created the next one.
            
    
            Deposit
            A shielded payment the holder made, opened by a sender receipt, paid from disclosed notes.
            
    
            Transparent payment
            A payment the holder made to a transparent address, from disclosed notes. Funds that come back from that address show as “returned from claim #k”.
            
    
            Control
            A challenge transaction spent disclosed notes and paid the holder a note whose memo carries your nonce: the holder could spend those funds after you issued it.
            
    
            Beacon nonce
            The hash of a block, zeceipt-beacon-<height>-<hash>. No one could know it before that block, so a control answering it was made after it; you judge whether the block is recent enough.
            
    
            H₀
            The chain height when you issued the nonce. A control transaction mined before it fails.
            
    
            Verified
            The chain supports the claim.
            
    
            Not proven
            Nothing in this dossier can show the claim, and waiting will not change that; its row says what would.
            
    
            Not checked yet
            A transaction the claim rests on was not found, or is not mined yet: check again later.
            
    
            Failed
            The chain does not support the claim, or its data does not check: its row says why.
            
    
            Not checked against the chain
            Checked against transaction files you loaded, not against a node: inclusion and heights are unknown.
            
    
            TAZ
            Testnet coins, which have no value; ZEC on mainnet.

    What the holder does
    
              
      
            1. You send the holder this nonce.
              2. 
      
            2. From the wallet that holds the funds, they send any amount (the smallest will do) to their own address, with the nonce as the memo. It must spend notes the dossier discloses.
              3. 
      
            3. They add that transaction to their dossier as the control claim (Build a dossier: Control), and send you the new dossier.
              4. 
      
            4. You open it here: the control claim must verify and answer this nonce.
              5. 
    
            
    
            The nonce, H₀ and address are kept in these fields only: reloading the page forgets them, so copy them into your case file. A case on screen is checked again when you change them.
