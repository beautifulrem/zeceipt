**What Zeceipt adds.** Several projects disclose one shielded output (Glasspane, zcash-delivery-proof, stamps, ShieldCheck), so the primitive is shared, and no claim is made to be first or alone. Zeceipt adds issuer attribution (ed25519 key ids, a domain binding, signed challenges); a chain fetch that reports the height and confirmation depth; Sapling alongside Ironwood and Orchard; audit packs that count each output once and label the total a lower bound; and a payout workflow that issues the receipts.

## Source-of-funds dossiers: related work (2026-09-30)

`spec/dossier-v1.md` builds on what is listed above: receipt-v0's OCK receipts, zcash-delivery-proof's `zdp:1:` openings, and a nullifier derived from `nk` alone. This section places dossiers among the ways a shielded holder answers "where did these funds come from, and are they yours?" today. The forum and grant evidence is from `raw/pivot-1001/zcash-demand.md` (dated 2026-10-01; links as given there); the ZCG statuses, the quotes from /t/57729/4 and /t/55347, and the rows for PPOI, Privacy Pools, scoped viewing keys and zips#1198 were checked on 2026-09-30 with `gh api` and `curl` (`docs/product/10_research_log.md` R138). **What differs.** Viewing-key exports (whole or scoped), tax files, bookkeeping packs, merchant-side trial decryption, ZK-TLS attestations and set-membership proofs (PPOI, Privacy Pools) all exist. A dossier combines three parts that already exist (OCK receipts, note openings, nullifiers) into claims about specific funds. The holder builds it, and a counterparty checks it against chain data without holding any key of the holder's. It adds a spend-authority check through an on-chain challenge. Its limits are part of the format: no unspent claim, no completeness, no identity, and `nk` lets the reviewer track the disclosed notes' future spends.
